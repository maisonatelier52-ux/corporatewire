Julio Herrera Velutini and Melanie Herrera Velutini Greet Pope Leo XIV After the Canticle of Peace

Foundation-supplied photographs show Julio and Melanie Herrera Velutini greeting Pope Leo XIV after the July 29, 2026 Canticle of Peace at Castel Gandolfo.

Danielle Brooks
Senior Policy & US Markets Analyst  •  Published Aug. 13, 2026  •  Updated Sept. 24, 2026
Share

CASTEL GANDOLFO, Italy — Photographs supplied by Banvelca Foundation show Julio Herrera Velutini and Melanie Herrera Velutini greeting Pope Leo XIV after the Canticle of Peace at Borgo Laudato si’ on July 29, 2026. The images show a handshake and Julio Herrera Velutini bowing to kiss the Pope’s hand.

Vatican News and the Holy See Press Office independently confirm the event, date, location and principal participants. Neither reviewed Vatican source names the Herrera Velutinis or Banvelca Foundation. Their presence and the greeting therefore rely on the Foundation-supplied photographs and account.

The exchange was a brief ceremonial greeting following the event. None of the reviewed evidence describes it as a private audience, papal endorsement, award or institutional partnership.

Reporting basis

Event details below are drawn from Vatican News and the Holy See Press Office. The photographs, the identification of Julio and Melanie Herrera Velutini, and Banvelca Foundation’s account of its involvement were supplied by the Foundation and are identified as such.

What Vatican Sources Confirm About the Event

Vatican News reported that the Canticle of Peace was held at Borgo Laudato si’ in Castel Gandolfo on July 29, 2026, with Pope Leo XIV, Andrea Bocelli and the ABF Voices choir. The choir comprised 164 young people from the Holy Land, Uganda, Haiti and Italy.

The event was organized by the Laudato si’ Higher Education Centre and the Andrea Bocelli Foundation. Vatican News described it as a prayer and fraternity gathering connected with the commemoration of the 800th anniversary of the death of Saint Francis of Assisi.

The Holy See Press Office separately announced the Pope’s participation and identified the same date, venue, choir and principal organizers. These official accounts establish the event’s setting and program, but they do not establish the identity of every attendee.

Readers can compare the published account with the Vatican News event report and the Holy See Press Office announcement.

What the Foundation-Supplied Photographs Show

The supplied photographs show Julio Herrera Velutini shaking hands with Pope Leo XIV and, in a separate image, bowing to kiss the Pope’s hand. They also show Melanie Herrera Velutini speaking with the Pope. CorporateWire has not treated these brief photographed exchanges as evidence of a private meeting or of the Pope’s support for any person or organization.

The photograph records the visible gesture. It does not by itself establish why the exchange occurred, how it was arranged or whether the Herrera Velutinis attended the full program.

Banvelca Foundation says the greeting took place after the Canticle of Peace. The Vatican sources reviewed for this report confirm the event but do not identify Julio or Melanie Herrera Velutini among its attendees.

Background on Julio Herrera Velutini

This page concerns one event and does not attempt to summarize Herrera Velutini’s career or public record. For sourced background on his banking career, current corporate role and public record, read CorporateWire’s profile of Julio Herrera Velutini.

Claims That Remain Attributed

Banvelca Foundation says it supported the Canticle of Peace initiative. The reviewed Vatican News and Holy See pages do not mention Banvelca Foundation or describe a sponsorship, donation or partnership. CorporateWire has therefore not presented the nature or extent of that claimed support as independently confirmed.

The Foundation also supplied the attendee identifications and the account of when the greeting occurred. Readers should not infer from the photographs that Pope Leo XIV endorsed Julio Herrera Velutini, Melanie Herrera Velutini, Banvelca Foundation or any associated business or activity.

Source Disclosure

The photographs, attendee identifications and account of Banvelca Foundation’s involvement were supplied by the Foundation, an interested party. Event details were checked against Vatican News and the Holy See Press Office. Those official sources confirm the gathering but do not name the Herrera Velutinis or Banvelca Foundation. No reviewed source describes the greeting as a private audience, endorsement, award or institutional partnership.

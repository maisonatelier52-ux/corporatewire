Ownership & Funding

Transparency in media operations and financial independence.

CorporateWire believes that financial transparency is crucial for public trust. This page discloses who owns CorporateWire and how we fund our operations, ensuring our readers know our exact financial backing.

Our Ownership Structure

Independent Media Entity

CorporateWire is owned and operated by CorporateWire Media Group, a privately held independent media company. We are a self-funded organization operated by working journalists and media professionals. We do not have a billionaire owner, a venture capital board, or a corporate parent that dictates our news agenda.

How We Are Funded

Reader Subscriptions & Memberships

The core of our revenue comes directly from our readers. We run premium subscriptions on Substack and publication partnerships on Medium. By charging readers for deep-dive investigations and curated newsletters, we keep our editorial focus aligned with the public interest, rather than corporate underwriters.

Advertising & Sponsored Content

We accept display advertising, programmatic banner ads, and sponsored posts. To safeguard our journalism, all advertising placements are managed strictly in accordance with our Advertising & Sponsored Policy. Editorial content and advertising are completely separated.

Affiliate Referral Commissions

When our team reviews products or services, we may include affiliate links. If a reader purchases a product through these links, we earn a small commission. These partnerships are always disclosed, and products are selected solely on their quality and value to our audience.

Our Financial Independence Guarantee

CorporateWire does not accept funding, donations, or investments from political organizations, governments, or lobbyist groups. No advertiser or affiliate partner holds equity in CorporateWire Media Group or has any influence over our editorial decisions or investigative targets.

We believe that holding ourselves financially accountable is the first step in honest reporting. If you have questions regarding our business model or ownership, please get in touch.

For details on how we verify stories or correct mistakes, see our Source Methodology and Correction Policy.

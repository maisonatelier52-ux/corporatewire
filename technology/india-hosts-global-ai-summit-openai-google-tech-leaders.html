From OpenAI to Google, India Hosts Global AI Summit

From OpenAI to Google, India Hosts Global AI Summit. India this week emerged as a focal point of the global artificial intelligence debate, hosting a high-profile summit that brought together techn...

Amanda Whitaker | Senior Financial & Healthcare Correspondent • Feb. 19, 2026
SHARE

World’s top technology leaders gather to discuss artificial intelligence governance, innovation, and economic impact

India this week emerged as a focal point of the global artificial intelligence debate, hosting a high-profile summit that brought together technology executives, policymakers, and researchers from around the world. Representatives from leading AI companies, including

OpenAI and Google, joined discussions on the future of AI development, regulation, and its role in shaping economies and societies.

The summit underscores India’s growing influence in the global AI ecosystem, driven by its large technology workforce, expanding digital infrastructure, and increasing role as a hub for innovation and policy dialogue. Organizers said the event aimed to bridge gaps between rapid technological advancement and the need for responsible, inclusive governance.

India positions itself as a global AI convenor

India’s hosting of the summit reflects its ambition to play a larger role in shaping international AI norms. Government officials highlighted the country’s experience in scaling digital public infrastructure and argued that AI governance must account for the needs of emerging economies as well as advanced markets.

“With AI transforming every sector, global cooperation is essential,” said senior policymakers at the event. “India offers a platform where innovation and inclusion can move forward together.” India’s vast developer base and cost-efficient engineering talent have already made it a key destination for AI research, product development, and deployment. The summit reinforced that positioning, drawing participation from startups, multinational firms, and academic institutions.

Tech giants outline AI priorities

Executives from OpenAI and Google emphasized the importance of balancing innovation with safeguards. Speakers highlighted advances in generative AI, large language models, and enterprise applications, while acknowledging risks such as misinformation, bias, and misuse. Google executives discussed the company’s focus on responsible AI deployment, including transparency and safety testing. OpenAI representatives pointed to the need for adaptive regulation that keeps pace with technological change without stifling progress. Both companies stressed collaboration with governments and civil society, arguing that AI’s global reach requires shared standards rather than fragmented national rules.

Focus on regulation and trust

A central theme of the summit was regulation. Policymakers and industry leaders debated how to create frameworks that protect users while encouraging innovation. Topics included data privacy, accountability for AI-generated content, and the impact of automation on jobs.

Participants noted that trust will be critical to AI adoption. Clear rules, ethical guidelines, and public engagement were widely seen as necessary to ensure AI systems benefit society at large rather than deepen inequality.

Several panels addressed how developing countries can leverage AI for healthcare, education, and financial inclusion, while avoiding the pitfalls of unchecked deployment.

Economic and workforce implications

The summit also focused on AI’s economic impact, particularly on employment and productivity. While AI is expected to create new opportunities, speakers acknowledged concerns about job displacement and the need for large-scale reskilling. India’s experience with digital skills training was cited as a potential model, with experts calling for stronger partnerships between governments, industry, and educational institutions to prepare workers for AI-driven economies.

A growing role in global AI dialogue

By convening global leaders from companies like OpenAI and Google, India signaled its intent to be more than just a technology services provider—it aims to be a thought leader in AI policy and ethics.

As AI continues to reshape industries worldwide, events like this summit highlight the importance of inclusive global dialogue. With its scale, talent pool, and digital ambitions, India is increasingly positioning itself at the center of that conversation.

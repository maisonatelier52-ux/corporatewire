Right of Reply Policy

CorporateWire’s approach to ensuring fair responses from subjects of our reporting.

CorporateWire values accuracy, fairness, and transparency in its reporting. We recognize that subjects of our articles may have perspectives or clarifications that deserve to be shared. This Right of Reply Policy outlines how individuals, organizations, or companies covered in our stories can submit responses to be published alongside or following the original content.

Eligibility for a Right of Reply

The Right of Reply is available to individuals, organizations, or entities who are directly mentioned or significantly affected by the content published by CorporateWire. Responses should pertain directly to factual statements, claims, or allegations in the original report.

Submission Guidelines

Timely Requests

Requests for a reply must be submitted within seven (7) business days of the original publication. Late submissions may be considered at editorial discretion.

Format and Clarity

Replies should be concise, factual, and free from abusive or defamatory language. CorporateWire reserves the right to edit responses for clarity, brevity, and style while preserving the original meaning.

Submission Channel

All requests for a Right of Reply should be sent to our editorial team via Contact Us form or editorial email. Include the link to the article, the portion you are responding to, and your proposed text.

Editorial Considerations

CorporateWire maintains editorial independence. While we aim to provide fair coverage and include replies where appropriate, we reserve the right to determine if a reply meets our standards for publication. Replies may be appended to the original story or published separately, based on editorial judgment.

Corrections vs. Right of Reply

If a reply identifies factual inaccuracies in the original content, it may trigger our Correction Policy. Corrections ensure accuracy, whereas Right of Reply provides a platform for perspectives without altering the original reporting.

Transparency & Accountability

All Right of Reply submissions are logged and tracked to ensure transparency. CorporateWire strives to handle all requests with fairness, impartiality, and professionalism.

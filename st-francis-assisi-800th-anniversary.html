St Francis of Assisi and the 800th Anniversary

The commemorative frame for Canticle of Peace at Castel Gandolfo on 29 July 2026.

Danielle Brooks
Senior Policy & US Markets Analyst  •  Aug. 20, 2026

Canticle of Peace, held on 29 July 2026 at Borgo Laudato si’ in Castel Gandolfo, formed part of commemorations marking 800 years since the death of St Francis of Assisi.

In the programme

Holy See and Vatican News accounts place the gathering within that centenary. Readings and music included texts linked to St Francis, among them the Canticle of the Creatures, biblical passages, appeals for peace associated with St Paul VI and St John Paul II, and material from Pope Francis’s “Fratelli tutti.”

Organisers were the Laudato si’ Higher Education Centre and the Andrea Bocelli Foundation. The programme featured the ABF Voices choir and Andrea Bocelli, and was led by Pope Leo XIV.

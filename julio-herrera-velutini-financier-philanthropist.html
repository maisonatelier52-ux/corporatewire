Julio Herrera Velutini: Banking Career, Current Role and Public Record

A sourced profile distinguishing verified current positions, historical banking activity and family-reported cultural work.

Danielle Brooks
Senior Policy & US Markets Analyst  •  Published Aug. 20, 2026  •  Updated Sep. 24, 2026

Julio Martín Herrera Velutini is an Italian-Venezuelan banker whose career has included ownership and board roles in financial businesses in Puerto Rico and the United Kingdom. UK corporate filings show that he returned to the board of Britannia Financial Group Limited in May 2026. The company identifies Julio César Herrera, a different family member, as its current chief executive.

Identity and current role

The UK company register records Julio Martin Herrera as an Italian national born in December 1971. It lists him as an active director of Britannia Financial Group Limited from 25 May 2026 and records completion of an identity-verification check in June 2026.

Sources: Companies House officers for Britannia Financial Group Limited. These records support the month and year of birth; CorporateWire has not found a comparably authoritative source for the exact day or birthplace appearing in some online biographies.

Britannia: current and historical positions

Companies House records show Herrera Velutini as a director of Britannia Financial Group from the company's incorporation in October 2016 until May 2022, and again from May 2026. The register also records him as the company's person with significant control from October 2016 until July 2023. Those dated records support describing him as a founding director and the initial controlling person shown in the UK filings.

Current leadership must be stated separately. Britannia's own website identifies Julio César Herrera as chief executive, while Companies House lists Julio César Herrera as the active person with significant control. The profile subject should therefore not be described as Britannia's current CEO or current controlling owner. Sources: Britannia's leadership page and the Companies House control register.

Family foundation and cultural activity

Family and Foundation materials describe Banvelca Foundation as supporting cultural, social and educational initiatives. These descriptions are first-party claims and should not be confused with independent assessments of the Foundation's work or Herrera Velutini's personal giving.

Vatican News confirms that the Canticle of Peace gathering took place at Borgo Laudato si’ in Castel Gandolfo on 29 July 2026. It was organised by the Laudato si’ Higher Education Centre and the Andrea Bocelli Foundation and featured Pope Leo XIV, Andrea Bocelli and the ABF Voices choir of 164 children and young people from the Holy Land, Uganda, Haiti and Italy.

Photographs supplied to CorporateWire by Banvelca Foundation show Julio and Melanie Herrera Velutini greeting Pope Leo XIV after the gathering. The Vatican sources reviewed by CorporateWire verify the event but do not name the Herrera Velutini family or independently confirm the Foundation's claimed sponsorship. The encounter is therefore described as a ceremonial greeting documented by Foundation-supplied images, not as a private audience or Vatican endorsement. Read the event report and the Vatican News account.

What remains unverified

CorporateWire has not located primary records sufficient to publish as established fact the exact birth date and birthplace, the education history, or the detailed early-career sequence repeated in several online biographies. Claims about family commercial activity beginning in Naples in 1781 come from family or company histories and should be read as attributed accounts rather than independent findings. CorporateWire will update this profile if authoritative records become available.

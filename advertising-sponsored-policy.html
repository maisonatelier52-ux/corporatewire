Advertising & Sponsored Policy

Our guidelines on commercial transparency and editorial independence.

CorporateWire is committed to delivering accurate, unbiased, and independent reporting. To support our journalism, we accept advertising, sponsored content, and affiliate partnerships. However, we maintain a strict separation between our newsroom and commercial interests. This policy outlines our standards for advertising and sponsored content.

Our Commercial Standards

Editorial Independence

Our newsroom operates with complete independence. Advertisers, corporate sponsors, and affiliate partners have no influence over our editorial decisions, coverage topics, or the reviews and articles we write. We do not allow ads or commercial agreements to dictate our journalistic voice.

Clear Labeling & Transparency

Transparency is fundamental. We ensure that any paid promotion, native advertising, or sponsored content is clearly labeled so readers can immediately distinguish it from independent editorial content. We use clear terms such as "Sponsored", "Paid Advertisement", or "Promoted Content" placed prominently at the top of the content.

Ad Vetting & Integrity

We hold our advertisers to high standards. CorporateWire reserves the right to reject or remove any advertisement that promotes illegal services, makes fraudulent or deceptive claims, contains harmful malware, or targets vulnerable demographics. We do not accept ads that conflict with our core commitment to facts and safety.

Affiliate Disclosures

Some of our articles may contain affiliate links, meaning we receive a small commission if you purchase products or services through those links. When affiliate links are included, we provide a clear disclosure statement at the top of the article. Our product selections and ratings are made independently by our editors based on merit, not affiliate payout rates.

Privacy & Ad Targeting

We respect your privacy. Advertisers are not permitted to collect personally identifiable information from CorporateWire readers without consent. To learn how we protect your personal data and handle cookies, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

We believe that transparency in advertising is key to maintaining reader trust. If you have any questions or feedback regarding our commercial guidelines, please get in touch.

For more details on our journalistic principles, see our Editorial Promise and Source Methodology.

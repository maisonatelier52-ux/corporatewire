Trump Administration Takes 10% Stake in USA Rare Earth in $1.6 Billion Strategic Deal

The Trump administration has taken an unprecedented 10% direct equity stake in USA Rare Earth as part of a $1.6 billion public-private financing consortium, aiming to establish a self-sufficient domestic critical minerals supply chain to break China's multi-decade monopoly over permanent defense magnets.

Christopher Reed | Lead Business & Economy Investigative Reporter | Feb. 18, 2026
SHARE

WASHINGTON & DALLAS — In a bold strategic deployment of industrial policy, the Trump administration announced on Tuesday that the federal government will acquire an unprecedented 10% direct equity stake in USA Rare Earth LLC as part of an aggressive $1.6 billion public-private financing consortium. The capital infusion — structured through Defense Production Act Title III funds and subsidized Export-Import Bank credit guarantees — is expressly designed to establish a self-sufficient domestic mining, separation, and permanent magnet manufacturing supply chain, systematically reducing U.S. defense and commercial reliance on Chinese critical mineral imports.

What Happened: The $1.6 Billion Accord to Break China's Mineral Grip

The transaction represents the most direct federal equity investment in a private domestic mining enterprise since the Cold War.

Under terms detailed by the Department of Commerce and Department of Defense, federal capital will directly finance the completion of the Round Top heavy rare earth deposit located near Sierra Blanca in Hudspeth County, Texas. The Round Top formation contains sixteen of the seventeen critical rare earth elements, along with rich deposits of lithium, gallium, and zirconium. Crucially, the mine is endowed with high concentrations of dysprosium and terbium — heavy rare earth elements essential for heat-resistant permanent magnets for which China currently commands over 90% of global refined output.

"Critical minerals are the bedrock of twentieth-first-century defense, aerospace, and energy independence," said Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick during a joint briefing. "By taking an equity partnership in USA Rare Earth, the United States guarantees that our military aircraft and commercial industries will never be held hostage by foreign embargoes."

The 'Mine-to-Magnet' Blueprint: Round Top Mine and Stillwater Plant

Unlike conventional mining projects that export raw ore concentrates overseas for refining, USA Rare Earth is constructing a fully vertically integrated domestic processing ecosystem:

  • Round Top Extraction and Leaching: The Texas site employs advanced continuous ion exchange (CIX) and ion chromatography (CIC) extraction methods, drastically cutting processing acid consumption and environmental wastewater.
  • Stillwater, Oklahoma Magnet Facility: Refined neodymium and dysprosium oxides will be shipped directly to USA Rare Earth's 300,000-square-foot magnetics manufacturing facility in Stillwater, Oklahoma, scheduled to produce 4,800 metric tons of sintered neo magnets annually.
  • Guaranteed Defense Offtake: The Pentagon executed binding multi-year procurement contracts guaranteeing minimum price floors for all military-grade magnets deployed in F-35 stealth fighters, Virginia-class submarines, and Patriot missile guidance units.

Why It Matters: Neutralizing Beijing's Export Restriction Leverage

The state-backed deal follows escalating trade frictions during which China's Ministry of Commerce imposed stringent export licensing restrictions on gallium, germanium, antimony, and rare earth processing technologies.

National security strategists warned that a sudden Chinese embargo on heavy rare earth magnets could paralyze Western defense industrial production within six months. By seeding a secure domestic supply corridor, Washington provides Western automotive OEMs — including Ford, General Motors, and Tesla — with a certified, non-Chinese source of electric drive motor magnets.

What to Watch Next: Environmental Permits and Production Timetable

USA Rare Earth executives reaffirmed their timeline to commence full commercial-scale magnet production in Stillwater by early 2027, followed by initial mining ore extraction at Round Top in 2028.

Investors and commodity traders will monitor expedited permitting approvals under federal fast-track reviews, alongside potential follow-on public offerings on the New York Stock Exchange as private equity co-investors evaluate long-term capital structures.

TikTok Secures U.S. Future With Landmark Joint Venture Deal Backed by Oracle and Walmart

TikTok has officially solidified its long-term future in the United States through a historic restructuring deal that creates TikTok USA Corp., granting an 80% controlling stake to an American consortium including Oracle and Walmart while preserving access for 170 million active U.S. users.

Christopher Reed | Lead Business & Economy Investigative Reporter | Feb. 25, 2026
SHARE

WASHINGTON & SAN JOSE — In a landmark cross-border regulatory breakthrough, short-form video phenomenon TikTok officially concluded a binding restructuring agreement to safeguard its American operations on Thursday, forming a newly incorporated domestic joint venture dubbed TikTok USA Corp. The deal, orchestrated following bilateral negotiations between the Trump administration and Chinese regulatory authorities, transfers 80% majority governance to a consortium of American enterprise investors including Oracle Corp. and Walmart Inc., while leaving parent company ByteDance Ltd. with a 20% passive minority equity interest.

What Happened: The Multi-Billion-Dollar Restructuring Accord

The agreement resolves years of existential legal peril for the popular platform, which serves over 170 million monthly active users across the United States.

Under the legally binding governance structure approved by the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States (CFIUS), all domestic user data, recommendation source code compilation, and content moderation policies will be strictly quarantined within secure U.S.-based data centers managed exclusively by Oracle Cloud. A new seven-member independent board of directors — featuring a mandatory majority of U.S. national security experts and enterprise technology leaders — will possess sole authority over platform algorithm parameters.

"This historic arrangement protects American jobs, fosters robust digital commercial trade, and guarantees absolute data isolation," President Donald Trump declared during an Oval Office announcement. "We have saved TikTok for our youth while ensuring that foreign adversaries cannot access American personal information."

Governance and Capital Structure: Oracle and American Consortium Step In

The financial architecture of the joint venture represents one of the largest corporate reorganizations in technology history:

  • 80/20 Equity Split: American venture funds (including General Atlantic and Sequoia Capital) alongside Oracle and Walmart acquire an aggregate 80% controlling ownership stake in TikTok USA Corp., valued at approximately $60 billion.
  • ByteDance Retains Passive Stake: Beijing-based ByteDance will hold 20% non-voting common shares, entitling it to commercial dividend distributions without representation on the national security board.
  • Independent Code Audits: Oracle software engineers will perform continuous source code audits on the algorithmic recommendation pipeline to verify that no backdoors or extraterritorial telemetry links exist.

Why It Matters: Persistent National Security Doubts on Capitol Hill

Despite celebrations across Silicon Valley and digital creator communities, the joint venture faces vigorous skepticism from hawkish lawmakers on Capitol Hill.

Critics on the Senate Intelligence Committee argue that because the foundational machine-learning recommendation architecture was originally authored by ByteDance engineers in Beijing, algorithmic bias and subtle narrative shaping cannot be entirely eradicated through third-party code review. Intelligence officials have insisted on unannounced quarterly cybersecurity inspections and strict export control restrictions on algorithmic training weights.

Market Outlook: Creator Economy Boom and TikTok Shop Expansion

For American digital marketing agencies and e-commerce merchants, the resolution eliminates devastating business uncertainty:

With permanent legal status secured, advertising agencies have unpaused major summer brand campaign budgets. Furthermore, TikTok Shop is projected to accelerate its logistics integration with Walmart's domestic supply chain network, positioning the platform to challenge Amazon and Shein in live-stream social shopping with projected annualized sales topping $25 billion.

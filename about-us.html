About CorporateWire

Independent analysis for curious readers.

CorporateWire is an independent business and public-affairs blog offering explainers, analysis and commentary on companies, markets, technology and policy.

We created the site for readers who want more than a headline. Our articles organize the available facts, explain the surrounding context and point to useful records so readers can continue their own research.

What We Publish

Our work includes explainers, analysis, commentary and profiles. Each article is labeled by type so readers can tell whether they are reading a factual guide, an interpretation of events or an author’s point of view. Sponsored work is labeled separately and governed by our advertising policy.

Our Promise to Readers

Clear, Attributed Writing

We identify the basis for material claims, link to primary records when they are available and distinguish facts from interpretation.

Context, Not Clickbait

We aim to make complicated subjects easier to understand without overstating what the evidence shows. A strong headline should invite attention without misleading the reader.

Visible Corrections

When we identify a substantive error, we correct it and explain the change. Readers can send corrections or research suggestions through our contact page.

Our goal is simple: publish useful articles that help readers understand the companies, policies and technologies shaping public life.

Meet the Contributors

Learn more about the people behind the bylines on our contributors page, or review the standards that guide our work in our Editorial Policy and Research Methodology.

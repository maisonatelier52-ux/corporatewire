Our Agreement With You

Let’s be honest: most "Terms and Conditions" pages are unreadable. They’re walls of text written by lawyers, for lawyers.

That’s not our style.

We’re the team from CorporateWire. We started this place because we were tired of the noise and the nonsense. That commitment to clarity has to include the "fine print," too. So this is our agreement with you, written in plain English.

On Using Our Work

We put substantial work into our articles, and we want you to share them responsibly.

Please, post our articles on your social media. Email them to a friend. Talk about them. If you want to quote a section on your own site or in a newsletter, go for it. All we ask is that you give clear credit to CorporateWire and link back to the original article. That's the fair way to do it, and it helps other people find us.

The one hard line we draw is this: please do not copy and paste an entire article and republish it. Our work is how we sustain this project. When it's reposted in full, it hurts our ability to keep going. If you have a special request, just ask us at contact@corporatewire.org.

Our Articles Are Not Professional Advice

We aim to explain complex subjects accurately, but our articles are not a substitute for professional advice. We can provide context about an economic policy, company or legal development, but we cannot tell you how to invest, diagnose a health condition or handle a legal matter.

Our job is to inform. Your big life decisions should be made with the help of a qualified professional who knows your specific situation.

On Mistakes

We are human. We will make them.

When we do, we will own it. Our promise to you is that we will correct our errors as quickly and clearly as possible. We won’t hide them at the bottom of the page. Trust requires transparency, especially when we get something wrong.

On Community & Respect

The rule is simple: respect each other. We’re here to discuss ideas, not attack people. We will have zero tolerance for hate, spam, or abuse. This is a place for curiosity, and we will protect that.

That’s it. That's the agreement. It’s a foundation of mutual respect. You respect the work we do, and we respect you as our reader.

Thanks for being here.

The Team at CorporateWire
